How reserving works

Three taps, then it's a conversation

There is no checkout here on purpose. You shortlist what you're after, tell the shop how you want to get it, and they come back to you with what they actually have and what it costs.

  1. 1

    Shortlist

    Tap “Add to shortlist” on anything that sounds right. It saves in this browser, so you can come back to it.

  2. 2

    Choose collection or delivery

    Collect from Station Road, or ask about local delivery if you can't get down.

  3. 3

    Send the request

    You get a reference code and a ready-written email. The shop confirms availability, price and a pickup time.

Before you set off

Common questions

Where exactly is the shop?

17 Station Road, Llanishen, Cardiff CF14 5LS — on the parade of shops in the village, a short walk from Llanishen rail station.

What time do you close?

Google Maps lists 17:00 Monday–Friday and 16:00 Saturday. For holiday changes, ring 029 2075 6793 and ask.

Can you put something aside for me?

That's exactly what the shortlist is for. Build your list, send it through, and the shop replies with what's in stock and the price before you travel.

Do I pay on this website?

No. Nothing is charged here. You pay in the shop when you collect, or the shop tells you how to pay if delivery is arranged.

Can you order something in specially?

Ask. Use the shortlist notes box to describe what you're after — a particular age, a name, a theme — and the shop will tell you whether they can get it and how long it takes.
